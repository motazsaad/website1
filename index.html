The Arabic Language

اللغة العربية

Discover one of the world's richest and oldest languages.

Overview

Arabic is a Semitic language spoken by over 400 million people worldwide. It serves as the official language across 25 countries in North Africa and the Middle East, and is the liturgical language of Islam.

Key Characteristics

  • Right-to-Left: Arabic is written and read from right to left using the Arabic script.
  • Cursive Script: Words are always written in cursive where letters connect to each other.
  • Root System: Most Arabic words are built from a base of three consonant letters (e.g., K-T-B relates to writing).
  • Rich Vocabulary: The language features vast synonyms and poetic expressions.

Common Expressions

  • Marhaban (مرحبا): Hello
  • Hala (هلا): welcome
  • Shukran (شُكرا): Thank you
  • Na'am (نعم) / Laa (لا): Yes / No
  • As-salamu alaykum (السلام عليكم): Peace be upon you
  • Alaykum Assalam (عليكم السلام): Peace be upon you (greeting back)
  • Salam (سلام): Peace
